# Evidence Standard

## Claim labels

- **OBSERVED** — directly seen in a primary source or permitted product interaction
- **SUPPORTED** — corroborated by credible independent evidence
- **INFERRED** — reasoned interpretation of evidence
- **SPECULATIVE** — plausible but weakly supported
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence

## Source tiers

A: direct observation, official docs/changelog/pricing, source/release  
B: credible research/technical publication, company/founder communication  
C: public user reviews, forums, community, issues  
D: aggregators, SEO summaries, unattributed claims

Important strategic claims should have two independent sources or `SINGLE-SOURCE` explicitly attached.

Never count syndicated copies of the same announcement as independent sources.
