# Monthly Strategy Review

Ask: **Are we still building the right product?**

Review the last month of verified signals/insights and produce: category movement, competitor positioning changes, feature convergence, user-pain movement, technology shifts, differentiation opportunities, strategic risks, opportunity portfolio changes, assumptions that need validation, and questions requiring human decisions. Avoid feature-by-feature churn; focus on direction. Save to `reports/monthly/YYYY-MM.md`.
