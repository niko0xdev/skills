# Idea Challenge

Input: one product idea or feature hypothesis.

1. Rewrite it as a user/problem hypothesis, not a solution claim.
2. Research WHY BUILD: user pain, demand signals, strategic fit, market timing, technical enablement.
3. Research WHY NOT BUILD: weak demand, existing substitutes, strategic distraction, commoditization, cost/complexity, adoption friction, contradictory user evidence.
4. Verify the strongest claims on both sides.
5. Produce at least one lower-cost alternative to the proposed feature.
6. Score the opportunity with `references/scoring.md`.
7. Return BUILD / PROTOTYPE / WATCH / REJECT plus the minimum next validation step.
8. Do not create an implementation issue without explicit approval.
