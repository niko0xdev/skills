# Competitor Research

1. Load prior competitor profile if it exists.
2. Establish official identity, positioning, pricing, docs, changelog, integrations, and public technical surface.
3. If product behavior matters and permitted access exists, run the Product Teardown role.
4. Gather user voice independently from company materials.
5. Compare current evidence to prior snapshot and build a timeline of material changes.
6. Run Evidence Verifier.
7. Write/update `competitors/<slug>/` with `profile.md`, `positioning.md`, `features.md`, `pricing.md`, `ux.md`, `user-feedback.md`, and `timeline.jsonl`.
8. Normalize strategically relevant changes into signals.
9. End with: strengths, weaknesses, likely strategic direction, unknowns, and implications for our product.
