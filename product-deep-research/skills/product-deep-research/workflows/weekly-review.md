# Weekly Review

1. Read all signals created since the previous weekly review.
2. Cluster duplicates and related events.
3. Promote meaningful clusters into insights.
4. Re-evaluate existing insights for counter-evidence.
5. Update opportunity scores only where evidence changed.
6. Write `reports/weekly/YYYY-Www.md`: executive summary, market changes, competitor movement, user voice, technology movement, emerging patterns, opportunity board, recommendations, human decisions required.
7. Update `state.json`.
