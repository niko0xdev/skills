# Trend Research

1. Define the alleged trend precisely and the decision it could affect.
2. Seek independent evidence across market, competitors, users, and technology where relevant.
3. Search for disconfirming evidence and alternative explanations.
4. Distinguish: event, repeated signal, emerging pattern, established trend.
5. Record dates so an old pattern is not mistaken for a new one.
6. Run Evidence Verifier.
7. Create/update an insight only when evidence supports a pattern.
8. State confidence, scope, likely duration, affected personas/product areas, and what would falsify it.
